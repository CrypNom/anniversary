Our Story

How two strangers turned into each other's favorite person.

how it began

The First Hello

It all began on a quiet morning when she went to the temple. She shared a simple Instagram story of it, never knowing it would change both of our lives. On a whim, I replied to her story with a simple message. What started as an ordinary reply quickly turned into a conversation that neither of us wanted to end. One message became another, minutes turned into hours, and without realizing it, that small moment became the beginning of something truly special. Looking back now, it's amazing how one simple reply to an Instagram story became the first chapter of our story.

getting closer

Getting to Know You

A few months later, our college held its welcome program—a day filled with excitement, laughter, and unforgettable memories. We perform a couple's dance in front of the entire college. Standing together on that stage was both nerve-wracking and exciting, but with each step, every bit of nervousness faded away. As the music played, we danced together, creating a memory that would stay with us forever. It wasn't just a performance; it was one of the first moments where we truly felt like we belonged beside each other.

falling deeper

Our Little Adventure

One day, we both came up with the same little excuse—we told our families we were going out with our friends. Instead, we met each other and spent the day together, just the two of us. We wandered through the museum, talking about everything and nothing, laughing at the smallest things, and making memories that only we would understand. It wasn't about where we went; it was about finally having time together without distractions. That simple date became one of our favorite memories—a day filled with smiles, stolen moments, and the quiet happiness of just being by each other's side.

Exciting

A date by the Lake

Another little adventure, another harmless excuse to spend time together. We both told our families we were out with friends, but our hearts already knew where we were going. We met at Naag Pokhari, where the peaceful water and quiet surroundings gave us the perfect place to slow down and enjoy each other's company. We walked, talked about our dreams, shared countless laughs, and cherished every moment as if time had stopped just for us. It wasn't an extravagant date, but it didn't need to be. With you beside me, even the simplest moments became the sweetest memories—ones I'll always hold close to my heart.

Like this we both have an uncountable stories.
